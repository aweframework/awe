Pixel Admin LESS (legacy)

The LESS files in this folder come from the Pixel Admin stylesheets that AWE vendored under less/libs/pixel-admin-less.
They are now AWE-owned LESS: only the files that the AWE build imports were kept (the demo, the pages, the rtl rules,
the unused widgets and the images no rule reaches were deleted), and the rules are unchanged.

The original files carried no license or copyright notice of their own. The copyright of the Pixel Admin theme belongs
to its authors, and this note keeps that provenance with the code.
