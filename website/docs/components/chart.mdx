A chart draws the rows of a query: lines, columns, areas, pies... You describe it in the XML of the screen with a `chart`, its
axes and one `chart-serie` for each series, and AWE draws it with Apache ECharts in the colours of its theme. Below is the real chart
of the AWE React client. Pick another type, stack the series, hover a point and change the attributes: the XML below follows.

<AwePlayground spec={chart} />

:::info The data is a fixture
In a screen the rows come from a query (`initial-load="query"`) and the server turns the `chart` elements into the model the client
draws. Here the rows are the sample revenue of an invented company, in thousands of euros, and the model is the one those elements
produce. Nothing is sent to a server.
:::

## Recipes {#recipes}

### Compare lines over time {#recipe-lines}

Lines with a `legend` and a shared tooltip show every series for the month under the mouse. Click a name in the legend to hide
its series.

<AweLive height="26rem" {...chartExample({type: 'spline', legend: true, sharedTooltip: true, title: 'Monthly revenue', subtitle: 'Thousands of euros', xLabel: 'Month', yLabel: 'Revenue (kEUR)'})} />

```xml
<chart id="monthly" type="spline" label="Monthly revenue" subtitle="Thousands of euros"
       initial-load="query" target-action="Revenue">
  <x-axis label="Month" type="category" />
  <y-axis label="Revenue (kEUR)" />
  <chart-serie id="software" label="Software" x-value="month" y-value="software" />
  <chart-serie id="services" label="Services" x-value="month" y-value="services" />
  <chart-serie id="support" label="Support" x-value="month" y-value="support" />
  <chart-legend enabled="true" />
  <chart-tooltip shared="true" suffix=" kEUR" />
</chart>
```

Each `chart-serie` reads two fields of the rows: `x-value` names the point and `y-value` is its height.

### Share of the year {#recipe-share}

A `donut` (or `pie`, or `semicircle`) draws one series: one slice for each row. The legend is shown by default on these types.

<AweLive height="24rem" {...chartExample({type: 'donut', dataLabels: true, title: 'Revenue of the year', subtitle: 'By product line'})} />

```xml
<chart id="share" type="donut" label="Revenue of the year" subtitle="By product line"
       enable-data-labels="true" initial-load="query" target-action="YearByLine">
  <chart-serie id="share" label="Revenue" x-value="line" y-value="revenue" />
</chart>
```

### Percent of the total {#recipe-percent}

`stacking="percent"` stacks the series and scales each point to 100%, so the bars show the mix, not the amount. `bar` draws them
horizontally.

<AweLive height="26rem" {...chartExample({type: 'bar', stacking: 'percent', legend: true, title: 'Revenue mix', xLabel: 'Month', yLabel: 'Revenue (kEUR)'})} />

```xml
<chart id="mix" type="bar" stacking="percent" label="Revenue mix" initial-load="query" target-action="Revenue">
  <x-axis label="Month" type="category" />
  <y-axis label="Revenue (kEUR)" />
  <chart-serie id="software" label="Software" x-value="month" y-value="software" />
  <chart-serie id="services" label="Services" x-value="month" y-value="services" />
  <chart-serie id="support" label="Support" x-value="month" y-value="support" />
  <chart-legend enabled="true" />
</chart>
```

## Events and actions {#events-and-actions}

Clicking, double clicking or right clicking a point writes its coordinates and series to the attributes of the chart and raises
the event (`click`, `double-click`, `context-menu`), so a [dependency](../api/dependencies.md) can filter another component with it.
Zooming (`zoom-type`) raises `zoom`. These examples draw and react to the mouse for real; the dependencies and the actions below
need a screen, so they are not run here.

* `filter`: reloads the chart from its query.
* `add-points`: adds points to a series without reloading it, for live data.
* `add-chart-series`, `remove-chart-series`, `replace-chart-series`: change the series of the chart.
