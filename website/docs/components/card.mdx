A card is a metric tile: a title, a big value with its unit, an icon, and a change with its trend. It can also show a progress
towards a target, a sparkline, a description, a footer and, in its body, any other component. Below is the real card of the AWE React
client. Change its attributes and the XML below follows; copy it into your screen.

<AwePlayground spec={card} />

## Recipes {#recipes}

The attributes of these cards are static values. On a real screen, `initial-load="query"` fills the card with the first row of a
query, and the fields of the query (`title`, `value`, `delta`...) take the place of the attributes.

### Key figure with its trend {#recipe-kpi}

With an `icon` the change goes to the footer, next to the `caption`. The arrow and the colour follow the sign of `delta`.

<AweLive
  components={{revenue: {attributes: {title: 'Monthly revenue', value: '84200', unit: '€', icon: 'lucide:euro', iconTone: 'success', delta: '12.4', caption: 'vs. last month'}}}}
  nodes={[{elementType: 'Card', id: 'revenue'}]}
/>

```xml
<card id="revenue" title="Monthly revenue" value="84200" unit="€" icon="lucide:euro" icon-tone="success"
      delta="12.4" caption="vs. last month" />
```

### Progress towards a target {#recipe-target}

`target` draws the progress of the `value` against it, as a bar or, with `progress-style="gauge"`, as a half ring.
A `chip` tells the status at a glance.

<AweLive
  components={{goal: {attributes: {title: 'Yearly sales goal', value: '412000', target: '500000', unit: '€', chip: 'On track', chipTone: 'success', progressStyle: 'gauge'}}}}
  nodes={[{elementType: 'Card', id: 'goal'}]}
/>

```xml
<card id="goal" title="Yearly sales goal" value="412000" target="500000" unit="€"
      chip="On track" chip-tone="success" progress-style="gauge" />
```

### Sparkline and a good trend that goes down {#recipe-sparkline}

`good-trend="down"` paints a falling change as good news (fewer incidents is better). `sparkline` is a JSON array of numbers drawn under the value.

<AweLive
  components={{incidents: {attributes: {title: 'Open incidents', value: '37', delta: '-8', deltaUnit: '%', goodTrend: 'down', sparkline: '[52,49,47,44,45,41,37]', sparklineType: 'line', caption: 'last 7 days'}}}}
  nodes={[{elementType: 'Card', id: 'incidents'}]}
/>

```xml
<card id="incidents" title="Open incidents" value="37" delta="-8" delta-unit="%" good-trend="down"
      sparkline="[52,49,47,44,45,41,37]" sparkline-type="line" caption="last 7 days" />
```

## Events and actions {#events-and-actions}

A card has no event of its own. It refreshes like any component with `initial-load`: the `filter` and `reload`
[actions](../api/actions.md) load its query again, `autorefresh` reloads it every few seconds and
[dependencies](../api/dependencies.md) can show, hide or change its attributes. Buttons placed in its body launch their own actions.
