A pivot table turns the rows of a query into a cross-tab: pick the attributes that make the rows and the columns, and an aggregation
(a count, a sum, an average...) fills each cell. Below is the real pivot table of the AWE React client, drawn with react-pivottable. The
controls set how it starts; you can also drag the attribute names between the rows and the columns of the table itself, and
pick another renderer or aggregator from its own menus.

<AwePlayground spec={pivotTable} />

:::info The data is a fixture
In a screen the rows come from a query (`initial-load="query"`). Here they are 64 rows of invented sales (region, product, channel,
quarter, units and revenue). The names of the renderers and aggregators are the ones of react-pivottable, in English.
:::

## Recipes {#recipes}

### Heatmap of the revenue {#recipe-heatmap}

The `Heatmap` renderer paints each cell with a colour that grows with its value: the strong regions and quarters stand out at a glance.

<AweLive height="22rem" {...pivotTableExample({rows: 'Region', cols: 'Quarter', renderer: 'Heatmap', aggregator: 'Custom Sum', aggregationField: 'Revenue'})} />

```xml
<pivot-table id="revenue" rows="Region" cols="Quarter" renderer="Heatmap"
             aggregator="Custom Sum" aggregation-field="Revenue"
             decimal-numbers="0" initial-load="query" target-action="Sales" />
```

### Share of the total {#recipe-share}

`Sum as Fraction of Total` shows each cell as a percentage of the whole table. The other fractions (of rows, of columns) are
in the aggregator menu.

<AweLive height="22rem" {...pivotTableExample({rows: 'Product', cols: 'Channel', renderer: 'Table', aggregator: 'Sum as Fraction of Total', aggregationField: 'Revenue'})} />

```xml
<pivot-table id="share" rows="Product" cols="Channel" renderer="Table"
             aggregator="Sum as Fraction of Total" aggregation-field="Revenue"
             decimal-numbers="0" initial-load="query" target-action="Sales" />
```

### Bars inside the table {#recipe-bars}

`Table Barchart` draws a bar behind each number, scaled to the largest value of its column. The `Custom *` aggregators (`Custom Sum`,
`Custom Average`...) format their numbers with `decimal-numbers`, `thousand-separator` and `decimal-separator`.

<AweLive height="30rem" {...pivotTableExample({rows: 'Product', cols: 'Quarter', renderer: 'Table Barchart', aggregator: 'Custom Sum', aggregationField: 'Units'})} />

```xml
<pivot-table id="units" rows="Product" cols="Quarter" renderer="Table Barchart"
             aggregator="Custom Sum" aggregation-field="Units"
             decimal-numbers="0" initial-load="query" target-action="Sales" />
```

## Events and actions {#events-and-actions}

The pivot table has no event of its own. Its attributes (`rows`, `cols`, `renderer`, `aggregator`) are the starting point: what the
reader changes in the table is kept in the component, and these actions change it from a screen.

* `set-pivot-sorters`: sets the order of the values of an attribute.
* `set-pivot-group-rows`, `set-pivot-group-cols`: change the attributes that make the rows and the columns.
* `filter`: reloads the table from its query. A server action: it is not simulated here.
