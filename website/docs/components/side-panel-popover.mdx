Both are [dialogs](./dialog.mdx) that do not cover the whole screen: a **side panel** is anchored to the left or right edge and keeps the
screen in view, a **popover** opens next to the control it belongs to. They open and close like a dialog (a button with a `dialog`
action, `close` and `close-cancel` actions) and close with their cross, Esc or a click outside. Press the buttons below: the playgrounds
run those actions.

:::note
Both are drawn over the whole page, outside the preview, so they follow the colour mode of the site and not the one of the
**Light / Dark** switch of the preview.
:::

## Side panel {#side-panel}

Use it for content the user consults or edits next to the screen: filters, the detail of a row, settings. The header and the footer stay
in view and the body scrolls between them.

<AwePlayground spec={sidePanel} />

With `header-grid` the header of a side panel shows the selected row of a grid (an avatar, a title, a subtitle and `header-tag` tags).
That identity header needs a grid with a selected row and is not part of this playground: see
[the identity header](../api/side-panel.md#identity-header).

## Popover {#popover}

Use it for short content linked to a control: a quick edit, a few options, a note. Its `anchor` is the id of that control (a button, a
criterion, a grid...) and `position` the side where it opens. It follows the anchor while the page scrolls.

<AwePlayground spec={popover} />

## Recipes {#recipes}

### Filters on the left {#recipe-filters}

A side panel on the left edge, narrow, whose `on-close="reject"` makes the cross, Esc and a click outside behave as *Cancel*:
the actions that were waiting for the panel are cancelled.

<AweLive
  components={{
    OpenFilters: {attributes: {label: 'Filters', icon: 'lucide:filter'}, actions: [{type: 'dialog', target: 'FiltersPanel'}]},
    FiltersPanel: {attributes: {label: 'Filters', position: 'left', maxWidth: '20rem', acceptOnClose: false}},
    Search: {attributes: {label: 'Search', placeholder: 'Name or email'}},
    OnlyActive: {attributes: {label: 'Only active customers', style: 'switch'}, model: {values: [{value: '1', label: 'Yes', selected: true}]}},
    Reset: {attributes: {label: 'Reset'}, actions: [{type: 'close-cancel', target: 'FiltersPanel'}]},
    Apply: {attributes: {label: 'Apply', style: 'btn-primary'}, actions: [{type: 'close', target: 'FiltersPanel'}]},
  }}
  nodes={[
    {elementType: 'Button', id: 'OpenFilters'},
    {elementType: 'SidePanel', id: 'FiltersPanel', elementList: [
      {elementType: 'Tag', type: 'div', style: 'panel-body', elementList: [
        {elementType: 'Criteria', component: 'text', id: 'Search'},
        {elementType: 'Criteria', component: 'checkbox', id: 'OnlyActive'},
      ]},
      {elementType: 'Tag', type: 'div', style: 'modal-footer', elementList: [
        {elementType: 'Button', id: 'Reset'}, {elementType: 'Button', id: 'Apply'},
      ]},
    ]},
  ]}
/>

```xml
<button id="OpenFilters" label="Filters" icon="lucide:filter">
  <button-action type="dialog" target="FiltersPanel" />
</button>

<side-panel id="FiltersPanel" label="Filters" position="left" max-width="20rem" on-close="reject">
  <tag type="div" style="panel-body">
    <criteria id="Search" component="text" label="Search" placeholder="Name or email" />
    <criteria id="OnlyActive" component="checkbox" label="Only active customers" style="switch" />
  </tag>
  <tag type="div" style="modal-footer">
    <button id="Reset" label="Reset">
      <button-action type="close-cancel" target="FiltersPanel" />
    </button>
    <button id="Apply" label="Apply" style="btn-primary">
      <button-action type="close" target="FiltersPanel" />
    </button>
  </tag>
</side-panel>
```

### Confirm over a control {#recipe-popover-confirm}

A popover above its anchor. Opening it from the button that needs the decision keeps the question next to the action.

<AweLive
  components={{
    Archive: {attributes: {label: 'Archive customer', icon: 'lucide:archive'}, actions: [{type: 'dialog', target: 'ArchivePopover'}]},
    ArchivePopover: {attributes: {label: 'Archive this customer?', anchor: 'Archive', position: 'top', maxWidth: '16rem'}},
    Keep: {attributes: {label: 'Keep'}, actions: [{type: 'close-cancel', target: 'ArchivePopover'}]},
    Confirm: {attributes: {label: 'Archive', style: 'btn-danger'}, actions: [{type: 'close', target: 'ArchivePopover'}]},
  }}
  nodes={[
    {elementType: 'Button', id: 'Archive'},
    {elementType: 'Popover', id: 'ArchivePopover', elementList: [
      {elementType: 'Tag', type: 'p', label: 'Its orders stay in the history.'},
      {elementType: 'Tag', type: 'div', style: 'modal-footer', elementList: [
        {elementType: 'Button', id: 'Keep'}, {elementType: 'Button', id: 'Confirm'},
      ]},
    ]},
  ]}
/>

```xml
<button id="Archive" label="Archive customer" icon="lucide:archive">
  <button-action type="dialog" target="ArchivePopover" />
</button>

<popover id="ArchivePopover" label="Archive this customer?" anchor="Archive" position="top" max-width="16rem">
  <tag type="p" label="Its orders stay in the history." />
  <tag type="div" style="modal-footer">
    <button id="Keep" label="Keep">
      <button-action type="close-cancel" target="ArchivePopover" />
    </button>
    <button id="Confirm" label="Archive" style="btn-danger">
      <button-action type="close" target="ArchivePopover" />
    </button>
  </tag>
</popover>
```

## Events and actions {#events-and-actions}

Both store the `open` and `close` events when they are opened and closed, so a [dependency](../api/dependencies.md) can react to them.
They are opened and closed with [actions](../api/actions.md) of the buttons, the same ones as a dialog.

| Action         | Description                                                                  |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `dialog`       | Opens the side panel or popover named in `target`                            |
| `close`        | Closes it and goes on with the actions that were waiting                     |
| `close-cancel` | Closes it and cancels the actions that were waiting                          |

With `on-close="reject"` the cross, Esc and a click outside act as `close-cancel`; by default (`accept`) they act as `close`. With
`deferred="true"` the data of the components inside loads the first time it opens. While it is open the keyboard focus stays inside, and
it goes back to the control that opened it when it closes.
